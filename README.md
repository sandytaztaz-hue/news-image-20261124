A 1024×1024 broadsheet front page summing up Tuesday 24 November 2026 in one image: 36 verified stories across 7 regions, 13 original drawings, cream paper with a navy top band and an ink masthead, and a realistic world map drawn from real latitude/longitude geography with filled continents on a light-blue sea and numbered pins at true positions, plus a DATE CHECK box naming the stories left off this page and why. Every picture on the page is drawn with code — no photos, no external images.

## What is on the page

- Masthead: "Tuesday 24 November 2026", Tuesday Edition, with the press-time line and an italic subhead of the three hero stories.
- Stat strip with the six Tuesday numbers: +33% / 24 / 258 / 99% / 48 / $2.5m.
- Regions: Americas 11 · Europe 11 · Asia 7 · Middle East 4 · Africa 1 · Oceania 1 · Global 1.
- The 3 heroes: Huang is back at Nvidia (Santa Clara), Ukraine claims a warship sunk off Libya (Benghazi), 24 dead in the Cikande factory fire.
- The 10 minis with their cities: New York, Zurich, Gaza City, Geneva, Patna, Copenhagen, Arunachal Pradesh, Hormuz, the night sky, Wellington — each with a verification tag (CONFIRMED / REPORTED / CLAIM).
- The map: 12 numbered pins at true positions, with the night sky (12) having no pin.
- ALSO ON 14–36: 23 more stories in brief.
- DATE CHECK: the stories left off and what is still to come.
- HOW WE CHECKED: CONFIRMED = 2+ outlets · OFFICIAL = on the record · REPORTED = one outlet · CLAIM = one side's account.
